Rift Runner

Traverse 12 cycles of the neon labyrinth. Collect chrono cores, dodge anomalies, and keep your energy stable. Trigger combos by chaining pickups!

Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Move
  • Space: Time Pulse (freeze anomalies)
  • E: Emit Energy Burst (clear nearby hazards)
  • Q: Warp (random safe hop)
  • R: Restart cycle
  • P: Pause / Resume
Objective: Reach the portal in each cycle before health collapses.

Pro Tips

  • Chain chrono cores for combo bonuses and faster energy gains.
  • Use Time Pulse before stepping into tight corridors.
  • Warp is safest when anomalies cluster near the portal.
